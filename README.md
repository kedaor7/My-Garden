# 🪴 My Garden (Catatan Tanaman)

A beautiful, feature-rich Progressive Web Application (PWA) designed to help farmers, hobbyists, and gardening enthusiasts manage their gardens effortlessly. Built with a modern, iOS-inspired interface, this app brings all your gardening needs into one place.

## ✨ Features

* **🏡 Garden Management:** Keep track of your garden plots, plant varieties, plant count, and land area.
* **🗺️ Interactive Garden Map:** Visually plot your plants on a virtual map. Supports multi-select, zooming, text labels, locking elements, and exporting your map layout as an image.
* **💰 Financial Tracker:** Monitor your income and expenses with customizable ledgers, categorized records, and flexible date filtering (Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, Custom).
* **📦 Inventory Control:** Easily manage your stock of fertilizers (Pupuk) and pesticides (Racun), tracking purchase dates, weights, and costs.
* **📝 Notes & Gallery:** Document your gardening journey with rich-text notes and a dedicated photo gallery for your plants.
* **🤖 AI Gardening Assistant:** A built-in chat interface to get instant gardening advice and tips.
* **📊 Smart Dashboard:** View real-time statistics, a dynamic weather widget, time/date, and quick activity summaries.
* **🌗 iOS-Native Feel & Dark Mode:** Enjoy a sleek, glassmorphism UI that automatically adapts to Light and Dark modes.
*  **👤 Profile:** You can add and customize your profile photo.
*  **⚙️ Account:** You can permanently delete your account, sign out, create new account, change password, change username.
*  **💾 Saving:** You can backup(export and import) your account data as .json file. This system using indexeddb and local storage, and can download your media on galery to save to your phone.
* **And many more features, check it out!**
  
## 🚀 How to Use

Since this is a client-side web application, running it is incredibly simple:
1. open https://kedaor7.github.io/My-Garden/ to your browser or chrome, then add or install to your home screen.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **HTML5 & CSS3:** Utilizing CSS Variables, Flexbox, Grid, and Glassmorphism effects for a native-app feel.
* **JavaScript (Vanilla):** For all application logic, state management, and UI interactions.
* **[html2canvas](https://html2canvas.hertzen.com/):** For rendering and exporting the interactive garden map to an image.
